# Privacy Policy

**Wordcard TTS** (`com.gh189.wordcard.tts`)
Developer: gh189
Effective date: October 24, 2026

[English](#english) · [中文](#中文)

## English

Wordcard TTS is an offline text-to-speech engine for Android. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

### Summary

Wordcard TTS does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal data.

### Data the app processes

- **Text to be spoken.** When you or another app asks Wordcard TTS to read text aloud, the text is turned into speech entirely on your device by the bundled voice models. Recently spoken audio is kept in memory for quick replay and is discarded when the app process ends. The text is never saved to storage or sent anywhere.
- **Diagnostic logs.** The app writes short diagnostic messages, which may include the requested text, to the Android system log on your device. These logs stay on your device, are not readable by other apps, and are never collected by the developer.

### What the app does not do

- It does not request any permissions, including internet access, so it cannot send data off your device.
- It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or third-party tracking services.
- It does not access your contacts, location, files, microphone, camera, or device identifiers.

### Third-party components

The app includes open-source components (sherpa-onnx, ONNX Runtime, eSpeak NG, piper-phonemize, and Piper voice models). They run locally on your device and do not collect data.

### Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

### Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this page with a new effective date.

### Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue at <https://github.com/gh189/wordcard-tts-oss/issues>.

## 中文

Wordcard TTS 是一款离线的 Android 文字转语音引擎。本政策说明本应用如何处理你的信息。

### 概要

Wordcard TTS 不收集、存储、分享或出售任何个人数据。

### 应用处理的数据

- **需要朗读的文字。** 你或其他应用让 Wordcard TTS 朗读文字时，文字完全在你的设备上由内置语音模型合成为语音。最近合成的音频暂存在内存中以便快速重播，应用进程结束后即清除。文字不会写入存储，也不会发送到任何地方。
- **诊断日志。** 应用会向设备上的 Android 系统日志写入简短的诊断信息，其中可能包含要朗读的文字。这些日志只保留在你的设备上，其他应用无法读取，开发者也不会收集。

### 应用不会做的事

- 不申请任何权限，包括网络权限，因此无法把数据发送到设备之外。
- 没有账号、统计分析、广告、崩溃上报或第三方追踪服务。
- 不访问通讯录、位置、文件、麦克风、摄像头或设备标识符。

### 第三方组件

应用包含开源组件（sherpa-onnx、ONNX Runtime、eSpeak NG、piper-phonemize 和 Piper 语音模型），它们都在你的设备上本地运行，不收集数据。

### 儿童

本应用不收集任何人（包括儿童）的个人信息。

### 政策变更

如本政策有变更，更新后的版本会发布在本页面，并注明新的生效日期。

### 联系方式

对本政策有疑问，请在 <https://github.com/gh189/wordcard-tts-oss/issues> 提交 issue。
